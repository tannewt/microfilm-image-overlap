TOWN TALK.

Gus Beck is preparing to cut a large
supply of ice.

Nine cars of stock were shipped
Saturday night.

Look for the advertisement of J. H.
Hess & F. A. Wood.

G. Holmberg is having an iron roof
put on his store building.

The hog market dropped thirty cents
per hundred during last week.

Will Hensel and his sister are keep-
ing house in the Grover building.

John Quackenbush will assist his
father at the Riverside this winter.

Mrs. John Steinberg, Abrahams
Cooley, gave birth to twins Wednes-
day.

This is pretty sloppy weather for
December, but the weather man says
cooler.

The funeral of Hans Johnson,
Ettrick, whose illness was mentioned
last week, was held Wednesday.
