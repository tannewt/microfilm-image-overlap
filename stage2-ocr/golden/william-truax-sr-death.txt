Died in His Bed.
William Truax, Sr. whose illness
was announced last week, died Monday
morning at the home of his son, Wy-
man, near Frenchville. Mr. Truax had
recovered sufficiently to move about.
He arose in the morning and called
the remainder of the household and
then returned to bed. Not appearing
later on, his son went to the bedroom
of his father. He found the elderly
man dead. It is probable that he died
soon after returning to his room. The
funeral was held Wednesday from the
Presbyterian church in this city, Rev.
Winder officiating. The deceased
leaves a wife and seven children. He
was an old resident of Galesville.
