# The Nelsons of the Galesville Newspaper OCR

A summary of the **Nelson** surname mentions in the OCR results
(`ocr_results/paddle/` — PaddleOCR output over the Galesville, Wisconsin newspaper
scans). About 190 raw hits were found; most are national news (Sen. Knute Nelson of
Minnesota, Gen. Nelson A. Miles, various Nelsons in election returns). The
Trempealeau-area individuals are covered first.

**OCR coverage (final):** the corpus is fully OCR'd — all 2,120 pages across the
three rolls (P81-4720: 673 pp., ~1895–96; P81-4721: 658 pp., ~1896–97;
P81-4722: 789 pp., ~1897–1900; rolls are year-mixed bindings, so dates
interleave). No further pages are pending.

**Note:** No "Anna Nelson" appears anywhere in the OCR (all "Anna <surname>" hits were
checked — Barlow, Bockenhauer, Bonum, Wentworth, Burmaster, Brady, etc., but never
Nelson), and no marriage between a Truax and a Nelson is reported in the pages OCR'd so
far. The OCR never gives **Mrs. David (Dave) Truax** a first name. If she was Anna
Nelson, the marriage likely predates the covered issues (a Truax son was born in 1895
and daughter Emma died in 1896 "aged nine," so the family was formed in the 1880s).

## Regarding the Truax–Nelson hypothesis

- **No "Anna Nelson"** appears in the OCR.
- **No Truax–Nelson marriage** appears in the OCR'd pages (1893–1900 coverage so far).
- The OCR never names **Mrs. David Truax** — she appears only as "Mrs. David/Dave
  Truax" (birth of a son in 1895; daughter Emma's death notice in 1896).
- Context: David Truax appears in Galesville village relief rolls in 1893–94, and the
  family (daughter Emma, b. ~1887) existed well before the newspaper coverage begins —
  so a marriage in the 1880s would never show up in these issues.
- If you want to test the Anna Nelson theory, the best OCR targets would be: (a)
  Trempealeau County marriage-license lists from the mid/late 1880s (not in this
  corpus), and (b) any "Mrs. David Truax" society items in the remaining un-OCR'd
  pages of P81-4721/4722 (still in progress).
